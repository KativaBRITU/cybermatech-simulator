❓ Frequently Asked Questions
How can I reset my password? ▼
Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password." Enter your email address and we'll send you a reset link.
Is Tribams free to use? ▼
Yes. Free accounts start selected modules. Paid plans are listed and charged in USD on PayPal. N$ on some screens is a Namibia display reference only. Pro unlocks the catalog; org seats are on /teams and /organization.
Which privacy law applies? ▼
TRIBAMS is established in Windhoek, Namibia. That is the governing law for the service. EEA and UK users have additional rights described in the Privacy Policy.
How do I earn certificates? ▼
Complete any module with a score of 80% or higher to earn a certificate. You can also earn cumulative certificates through daily streaks.
Can I get a demo for my organisation? ▼
Yes. Request a demo or USD quote on the For teams page, or email tribams@gmail.com.
