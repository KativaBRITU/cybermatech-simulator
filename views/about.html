Who We Are

Building Africa's cyber resilience through practical cybersecurity training and education.

🌍 Our Story

Tribams was founded in 2025 with a simple but powerful vision: to make world-class cybersecurity training accessible to every African professional, student, and organization.

We saw a gap between the growing cyber threats facing Africa and the lack of affordable, relevant, and practical training. Traditional courses are expensive, outdated, and often ignore WhatsApp-first fraud, mobile-money risk, ministry BEC, mining/port OT exposure, and African data-protection duties.

That's why we built Tribams (tribams.com) in Namibia — a practical training platform rooted in Windhoek and designed for Africa, by Africans.

🛡️

🎯 Our Mission

To empower individuals and organizations across Africa with the cybersecurity skills they need to protect themselves, their data, and their communities in an increasingly digital world.

We believe that cybersecurity is not a luxury — it's a necessity. Every organization, from small businesses to large enterprises, deserves access to high-quality, practical training that prepares them for real-world threats.

Our mission is to close the cybersecurity skills gap in Africa, one training module at a time.

🎯

💡 Our Values

Teamwork: We believe the greatest challenges in cybersecurity are solved together. Our strength lies in our collaborative spirit and shared knowledge.

Honesty: We are committed to radical candor — providing clear, truthful feedback to our clients and to each other. Our honesty builds trust.

Transparency: We operate with an open-book philosophy. Our clients see a clear view into our platform's capabilities and processes.
