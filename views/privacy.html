🔐 Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use the TRIBAMS platform.

Introduction

Overview

Welcome to TRIBAMS. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you visit our website, use our platform, or interact with our services.

By using TRIBAMS, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data with transparency and care.

💡 Key Point: We only collect information that is necessary to provide you with our training services and to improve your learning experience.
2

Information We Collect

Data

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our services:

  • Account Information: When you register, we collect your name, email address, username, and password (hashed with bcrypt).
  • Profile Information: You may optionally provide a profile picture, bio, and other personal details.
  • Training Data: We collect quiz scores, module progress, certificates, badges, and essay answers you submit.
  • Essay answers: Stored privately on your account for scoring and progress. They are not sold or published as training content for other learners.
  • Beta tester accounts: Issued closed-beta credentials may be flagged as beta testers to grant full module/resource access for product testing (not admin access).
  • Usage Data: We collect information about how you interact with the platform, including pages visited, features used, and time spent.
  • Device Information: We collect information about the device you use to access the platform, including browser type, operating system, and IP address.
  • Communication Data: If you contact us, we collect information about your communication with us.
  • Sessions & cookies: We use session cookies to keep you signed in securely.
⚠️ Important: We do not collect sensitive personal information unless you voluntarily provide it. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
3

How We Use Your Information

Purpose

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Provide and Maintain Services: To create and manage your account, deliver training modules, grade essays and quizzes, and track your progress.
  • Improve and Personalize: To personalise your learning experience, recommend relevant modules, and improve platform features — without publishing your essay text to other learners.
  • Communication: To send you updates, notifications, password resets, certificate notices, and responses to your inquiries (via email/SMTP when configured).
  • Payments: To process paid subscriptions through PayPal when you choose a paid plan.
  • Analytics: To analyse usage patterns and improve our platform's performance and user experience.
  • Security: To detect, prevent, and address security issues and fraudulent activity.
  • Compliance: To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms of service.
4

Cookies & Tracking

Preferences

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on our platform. Cookies are small data files stored on your device.

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for the platform to function properly (e.g., authentication, session management).
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your preferences and settings.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how you use the platform and improve our services.
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to deliver relevant advertisements (only with your consent).

You can manage your cookie preferences in your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect your experience on the platform.

5

Data Sharing & Disclosure

Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share your data in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted providers who help operate the platform — for example hosting (PostgreSQL or SQLite database), Cloudflare DNS/HTTPS/WAF in front of our Node origin, PayPal for payments, and SMTP email delivery. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, property, or safety.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your data may be transferred. You will be notified of any such changes.
💡 Note: Essay answers are not shared as public study notes or labs for other learners. We only share the minimum amount of data necessary for the specific purpose and require third parties to maintain adequate security measures.
6

Data Security

Protection

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Our security measures include:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using SSL/TLS.
  • Access Control: Access to personal data is restricted to authorised personnel who require it for their job functions.
  • Secure Storage: We store data on secure servers with firewalls and intrusion detection systems.
  • Regular Audits: We conduct regular security audits and vulnerability assessments.
⚠️ Important: While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.
7

Your Rights

Control

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: You have the right to correct any inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Erasure: You have the right to request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal obligations.
  • Restriction: You have the right to restrict the processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Portability: You have the right to receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Objection: You have the right to object to the processing of your data for certain purposes.
  • Withdraw Consent: You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

8

Data Retention

Storage

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to provide you with our services and for legitimate business purposes.

We may retain your data:

  • As long as your account is active.
  • To comply with legal obligations (e.g., tax, audit, or regulatory requirements).
  • To resolve disputes and enforce our agreements.

After your account is closed or terminated, we will retain your data for a reasonable period to ensure compliance with legal obligations and then securely delete it.

9

Children's Privacy

Minors

Our platform is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us. We will take steps to remove that information from our systems.

🚨 Important: Users must be at least 13 years old to create an account. Users under 18 require parental or guardian consent.
10

International Transfers

Location

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. These countries may have data protection laws that are different from those in your country.

We take appropriate measures to ensure that your data is protected in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable laws, including the use of standard contractual clauses where required.

11

Third-Party Links

External

Our platform may contain links to third-party websites or services that are not owned or controlled by us. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those websites.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

12

Changes to This Policy

Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
