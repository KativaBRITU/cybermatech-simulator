1

Who we are

Operator

TRIBAMS (tribams.com) is a cybersecurity education product: modules, in-browser Evidence Workbench labs, scored drills and essays, account-bound certificates, organisation seat licences, and USD checkout. The operator’s establishment is Windhoek, Namibia. We train individual learners and institutions worldwide.

This policy describes the records this service actually stores. It is not legal advice.

2

Governing law

Jurisdiction

TRIBAMS is established in Namibia. This Privacy Policy and the related Terms are governed by the law of the Republic of Namibia. Courts of Namibia have primary jurisdiction.

We process personal data to operate an education service: accounts, training records, certificates, and payments. Namibian constitutional privacy protections, electronic-transactions rules, and communications regulation apply as they do to any Windhoek-based online service.

For users in the EEA or United Kingdom, we additionally honour the access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and objection rights described in section 7. That commitment does not change Namibian governing law.

3

What we collect

Inventory

Without an account: contact and team-quote forms (name, email, company, message); optional health-check leads; ordinary web-request logs used to operate and secure the service.

With an account: username, email, password hash, optional profile picture, subscription status, quiz and lab scores, essay answers (private to your account for scoring), certificates, badges, organisation membership, and exam-integrity signals such as timing or focus-loss flags (not camera proctoring).

Payments: PayPal order and capture references, plan, amount in USD, and expiry. We do not store full card numbers. Manual plan requests and licence keys are stored so operators can activate the correct account after payment is confirmed.

4

How we use it

Purpose
  • Run your account, modules, labs, ranks, certificates, and org seats.
  • Process USD PayPal upgrades and B2B license requests.
  • Send operational email (password reset, support replies) when SMTP is configured.
  • Answer Matte K questions. If an OpenAI API key is configured on the server, your chat text may be sent to that processor to generate a reply; scored drills still lock Matte K.
  • Keep the service secure (session auth, integrity flags, rate limits, short-lived ops logs).
  • Reply to sales and contact messages you send us.

We do not run third-party advertising networks on this product, and we do not use marketing cookies.

5

Cookies, sessions, and local preferences

Honest

TRIBAMS does not set advertising or analytics cookies. The sign-in cookie is required for a logged-in session.

  • tribams.sid — essential session cookie (HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, Secure on HTTPS, 24-hour maximum age).
  • Display preference — the pricing page may store USD/N$ display choice in your browser’s localStorage. That stays on your device.
  • Fonts — some pages load the Inter typeface from Google Fonts. That is a third-party font request, not an advertising profile we build.

Detail: Cookie notice.

6

Sharing and processors

Transfers

We share the minimum needed to run the product:

  • PayPal — USD payments. Card data is handled by PayPal, not stored on TRIBAMS.
  • Email / SMTP — password resets and operational mail when SMTP is configured.
  • Hosting and database — the application origin and, in production, hosted PostgreSQL. The site may sit behind Cloudflare for DNS and HTTPS.
  • OpenAI — only if the operator has configured an API key for Matte K coaching. Scored assessments still lock that assistant.
  • Organisation administrators — if you join a licensed team, owners can see roster progress.
  • Lawful requests — where Namibian law requires disclosure, or to protect the service from abuse.

Enterprise buyers who need a signed data-processing agreement should email tribams@gmail.com.

7

Your rights

Control

Everyone with an account can:

  • Access and correct username, email, photo, and password on Profile.
  • Export a JSON copy of training and related account records from Profile (password hashes are omitted).
  • Delete the account and training rows from Profile, or by writing to us. Operator admin accounts listed for platform operations cannot self-delete from that form.
  • Contact us via Contact or tribams@gmail.com.

EEA and UK users may also request restriction of processing, objection to optional processing, and a portable copy of data they provided. We aim to respond within 30 days. Certificates you already shared may remain visible until deletion completes. PayPal keeps its own payment records.

8

Retention

Storage
  • Account & training: while the account exists; deleted when you (or an operator) purge the user.
  • Sessions: cookie max-age 24 hours; server store is memory (default) or files under database/sessions if configured.
  • Ops logs (process_monitor): pruned on a roughly 7-day window in the running process.
  • Reset / verify tokens: short-lived (password reset is on the order of one hour).
  • PayPal / tax: payment processor and any bookkeeping copies we must keep under Namibian commercial practice may outlive the training account.
9

Learners under 18

Minors

TRIBAMS is workplace and professional cybersecurity education. It is intended for adults (18+).

If a school or employer enrols a learner aged 16 or 17, that organisation (or a parent or guardian) must have authority to enrol them. We do not knowingly collect accounts from children under 16. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete the account.

10

Cross-border processing

Global

Checkout is in US dollars. Processors (PayPal, email, hosting, optional AI coaching) commonly operate outside Namibia. We use TLS in transit, hashed passwords, and access control. EEA/UK organisations that need a signed transfer addendum should request it by email.

11

Public pages, security posture, and other sites

Limits

We use TLS, HttpOnly session cookies, bcrypt password hashes, and admin-gated destructive actions. Training pages may link to external regulators or vendors; those sites have their own policies.

Updates

We will update this page when our practices or processors change, and we will change the date above. Continued use after a posted change means you have seen the new text.
