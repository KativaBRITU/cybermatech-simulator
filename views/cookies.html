Cookies

TRIBAMS uses an essential session cookie to keep you signed in. We do not set advertising or analytics cookies.

What we set

NameWhat it isCan you refuse it?
tribams.sid HttpOnly session cookie (express-session). SameSite=lax, Secure on HTTPS, 24h. Keeps you signed in, including PayPal return. Only by not signing in, or clearing cookies (which signs you out).
localStorage tribams_currency / tribams_nad_per_usd Payment page display: USD list vs Namibia N$ reference. Stays in your browser. Yes — clear site data. Checkout remains USD.

What we do not set

  • No Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Hotjar, or similar marketing cookies.
  • No “accept all” banner, because those cookies are not used.
  • We do not sell personal data collected via cookies.
Many pages load the Inter typeface from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). That is a third-party font request, not a TRIBAMS ad cookie. Browser extensions can block it; the layout still works with system fonts.

Governing law

TRIBAMS is established in Windhoek, Namibia. Session cookies are required to operate a logged-in education service. EEA and UK users have the additional rights described in the Privacy Policy.
