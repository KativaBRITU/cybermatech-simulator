# Stop rule — post-Step-10 review

**Decided:** 2026-11-04

At the end of Step 10 (batch rewrite), within 90 days of that step completing,
the founder must write a one-page go/no-go assessment against these three conditions:

1. At least 3 independent reviews are live (not just the first review from Step 13)
2. At least 5 paying customers from the free tier
3. The post-Step-12 independent security audit shows no unresolved criticals

If all three pass: continue with business track.
If any one fails: write a one-page assessment of why, and choose: continue / pivot / stop.
The decision must be written, dated, and committed to the repo.
Continuing without a written decision is not allowed.
